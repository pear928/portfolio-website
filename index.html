I'm a rising senior at Archbishop Mitty High School interested in AI safety at the intersection of cognitive science, machine learning, and human-AI alignment. My technical work includes research on model transparency and explainability, highlighted through my paper's acceptance to the 2026 International Conference on Machine Learning and my role as a research assistant at Stanford's Deliberative Democracy Lab. Alongside technical work, I've extended my work into the community, conducting interactive workshops on AI safety for over 400 Bay Area students in addition to district-level AI policy projects. These experiences, coupled with my #2 national ranking and discussions surrounding philosophy while competing in Lincoln-Douglas Debate, have encouraged me to think critically about the moral and ethical implications of technology and view AI safety as not only a technical challenge, but as one with real consequences for people and society.
