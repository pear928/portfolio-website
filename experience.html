ERODE: A Trajectory-Based Benchmark for Measuring Compliance Drift in Role-Embedded LLM Agents

Developed the ERODE benchmark under UC Irvine Professor Nawab, testing whether LLMs become more compliant to harmful instructions when embedded in institutional roles under escalating pressure. Found that safety guardrails erode gradually across turns even when no single step looks obviously harmful, modeled mathematically using Riemann sum approximations of harm accumulation functions. Mirrors dynamics from the Milgram Experiment and the "banality of evil" theory, and exposes a gap in existing AI safety research that only evaluates models in single-turn or overtly adversarial settings. View code on GitHub.
